Marina di Modica
Blue Flag

Marina di Modica is the seaside district of the municipality of Modica, overlooking the Ionian Sea about 18 km from the UNESCO historic center. Its lava stone promenade—one of the most characteristic on the Iblean coast—stretches for over a kilometer, lined with palm trees, benches, a cycle path, and a playground for children. In summer, it becomes the main hub for locals and tourists who want to combine a visit to the Baroque city with a day at the beach.

The sea at Marina di Modica alternates stretches of fine sand with flat limestone cliffs highly appreciated for their natural shallow waters and snorkeling opportunities. The water is particularly clear thanks to its sheltered position from the coast and the almost complete absence of industries in the hinterland. The Blue Flag, awarded annually by the Foundation for Environmental Education, certifies both the quality of the water and the level of seaside services.

Beaches and bathing establishments
free and equipped

The main beach extends in front of the promenade, with sandy sections and sections of low cliffs. The equipped bathing establishments offer sunbeds, umbrellas, showers, bars, and restrooms; they are generally open from 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM from June to September. The free beach occupies large stretches between the establishments, accessible for free without reservation.

North of the promenade, you'll find the rocky area: flat limestone rocks that gently slope into the sea, ideal for those seeking tranquility, for children who want to snorkel in shallow waters, and for fishermen who stay until sunset with their rods. This is the less touristy and most authentic part.

Practical tip: in July and August, arrive by 9:00 AM to find a spot on the free beach near the promenade. Parking fills up quickly on sunny mornings.

When to go
May – October

The sea at Marina di Modica is suitable for swimming from May to October. The water temperature gradually rises: around 20°C in May, reaching 26–28°C in August, and remaining pleasant until late October with 22–23°C.

June and September are the ideal months: the sea is already warm (23–25°C), the crowds are limited compared to August, and accommodation prices in Modica are more affordable. July and August are lively and bustling but crowded—the promenade becomes an evening gathering spot for the entire Iblean coast.

How to get there from Modica
~20 minutes

From Modica center to Marina di Modica, it's 18 km. The fastest route is the SS115 towards Pozzallo: the signs for Marina di Modica are clear, and the route is straightforward. Modica center is about a 20-minute drive with normal traffic; in August, during peak Saturday and Sunday morning hours, allow a few extra minutes.

Parking is free on the side streets off the promenade, a 5–10 minute walk from the beach. There is no organized paid parking: these are public roads. In high season (July–August), it fills up by 9:30 AM; out of season, you can easily find a spot at any time.

In the summer, a bus service operates from the city: the SAIS line n. 11 departs from the Terminal in Piazza Borsellino with departures approximately every 2 hours from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM, with late-night services in August until 3:50 AM. The ticket costs around €2.50. For updated schedules and all lines from Modica → consult the Transport page.

⚠️
The bus to Marina di Modica operates only during the summer season (July–September). Outside this period, there are no regular public transport connections: a car is essential.

What to do in Marina di Modica

Besides the sea, the promenade of Marina di Modica is lively in the summer with bars, ice cream shops, and restaurants serving fresh fish. In the evening, the promenade becomes a place for strolling, with families, young people, and tourists walking among the palm trees until late.

The most popular combination for visitors is "sea in the morning, Baroque in the evening": a few hours at the beach in Marina, then return to Modica in the late afternoon for a visit to the Cathedral of San Giorgio and dinner with typical dishes—pasta alla Norma, mpanatigghe, and of course, IGP chocolate. The 20-minute distance makes this combination perfectly feasible even without a car, thanks to the summer bus service.

Those who wish to explore the Iblean coast can extend their day to Sampieri (28 km, famous for the Fornace Penna from Montalbano film sets), Maganuco (22 km, more tranquil), or Cava d'Aliga (30 km, long sandy beach). For a complete overview of the beaches, see the guide to beaches near Modica.

Frequently Asked Questions
Does Marina di Modica have the Blue Flag?
Yes. Marina di Modica has received the Blue Flag for the quality of its seawater and bathing services, an annual recognition from the Foundation for Environmental Education.
Is there a bus from Modica to Marina di Modica?
Yes, in the summer. The SAIS line n. 11 departs from Piazza Borsellino every 2 hours from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM (July–September), with late-night services in August until 3:50 AM. Ticket ~€2.50. For all schedules → Transport page.
Are there free beaches?
Yes, large free stretches alternate with bathing establishments. The rocky area to the north is completely free and excellent for snorkeling.
Are there restaurants and bars?
Yes, in summer the promenade is bustling with bars, ice cream parlors, and fish restaurants. In the low season, the options are limited: it's better to dine in Modica center where establishments are open year-round.
Where can I park?
Free parking is available on the side streets off the promenade. In July–August, it fills up by 9:30 AM: arrive earlier. See also the Modica parking guide.
